Yi Huang

is a research-based literary and art worker.

Born in 2002 and based between Changsha and London, his practice examines the digital age, art history, and geopolitics through moving image, installation, and text.

He is currently building Confucian Silence, an atlas of East Asian manufacturing, alongside its fully automatic Semiconductor Daily.

He holds a BA in Digital Media Art from Xi'an Academy of Fine Arts (2020–2024) and an MA in Contemporary Art Practice from the Royal College of Art (2025–2026).
